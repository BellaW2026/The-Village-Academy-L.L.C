Frequently Asked Questions

What families ask before enrolling.

What is a hybrid microschool?
A hybrid microschool combines the small, community-centered feel of a microschool with flexible attendance options. Students can attend fully in person, complete their coursework independently through our virtual track, or split their week between the two — all while following the same individualized instruction plan.
What grades do you serve?
The Village Academy currently serves students in grades 1 through 4, organized into two tiers: Lower Primary (grades 1–2) and Upper Primary (grades 3–4).
What's the difference between the in-person and virtual tracks?
In-person students attend school on campus in Maricopa Monday through Thursday, 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM, in a small, mixed-age cohort. Virtual students follow the same individualized plan and materials, but complete their work independently on their own schedule, with regular check-ins from their teacher rather than live, real-time class sessions. Families can mix the two into a hybrid schedule.
What are the in-person school days and hours?
On-campus students attend Monday through Thursday, 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM. There is no on-campus instruction on Fridays.
Do you accept ESA funding?
Yes. Families may use an Arizona Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) through ClassWallet, or pay tuition privately. We provide itemized, ClassWallet-ready invoices on a predictable schedule for ESA families.
What does an "individualized instruction plan" actually mean?
Every student's plan is mapped to their own pace and needs rather than a single grade-level curriculum. That includes custom curriculum mapping, quarterly plan reviews, and instruction that adjusts as your child grows — whether they're ahead, on pace, or need more support in a specific area.
How do I enroll my child?
Start with our Admissions application. We'll follow up to talk through your child's needs, walk through tuition and payment options, and help you choose the right track.
Can siblings enroll together?
Yes, and sibling households receive a discount on tuition. Siblings can be enrolled in the same or different tracks depending on each child's needs.
I already homeschool my child — can you just help with curriculum?
Yes. Our Curriculum Building service is designed for families who want to continue homeschooling independently but would like a certified teacher to build the plan. We create an individualized curriculum — assignments, materials, and pacing — organized for you inside Google Classroom, and update it as your child progresses. You lead the day-to-day teaching; we build the roadmap behind it.
