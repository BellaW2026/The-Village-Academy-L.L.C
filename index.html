What every family gets

A learning plan built around your child, delivered however your family needs it.

  • An individualized instruction plan for every student
  • Choice of in-person, virtual, or a hybrid mix of both
  • A certified Arizona teacher leading every classroom
  • Small, mixed-age cohorts instead of large classrooms
Our Model

One private school, two ways to attend.

Families choose the track that fits their week — and can move between them as needs change throughout the year.

In-Person

On-Campus Microschool

Small, mixed-age cohorts meet in person at our Maricopa location for full-day instruction, hands-on projects, and community-centered learning.

  • Monday–Thursday, 8:30 AM–3:00 PM
  • Individualized pacing within a small cohort
  • Hands-on projects and peer collaboration
Virtual

Independent Virtual Track

Students work through the same individualized plan as our in-person students, on their own schedule, with materials and check-ins from their teacher — not live, real-time class sessions.

  • Self-paced learning on your family's own schedule
  • Same individualized curriculum plan as on-campus students
  • Regular check-ins with a certified teacher to review progress
Our Philosophy

Education rooted in advocacy, not one-size-fits-all.

The Village Academy was founded on the belief that every student deserves a learning environment shaped around who they are. That means an individualized instruction plan for every child, inclusive leadership, and a community built on real relationships.

01

Individualized Instruction

Every student learns on their own plan — paced, sequenced, and adjusted around how they actually learn best.

02

Inclusive Leadership

Learners of every pace and background are met where they are, with instruction that flexes instead of a child who has to.

03

Relationship-Driven Schooling

Consistency and trust come first. The same teacher, the same rhythm, the same community — week after week.

Founder & Head of School

Bella Washington

Bella founded The Village Academy to give Maricopa families a private school alternative built on trust and individualized instruction. She's an Arizona Certified Teacher with a Bachelor of Education in Elementary Education from Arizona State University, graduating Summa Cum Laude.

Before launching the Academy, Bella served as Head of School and Lead Teacher at a Maricopa microschool and taught in Maricopa Unified School District #20, where she received a District Spotlight Recognition for her work with students.
